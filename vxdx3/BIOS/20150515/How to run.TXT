
1. Extract the attached ZIP and then copy all files and BIOS ROM upon a DOS bootable of USB mass storage.
2. Plug that USB mass storage on VDX3 Board.
3. Power on and then run "BIOSMP -U XXX.ROM" in DOS.
4. After Finishing, please power off.
5. Power on again and then press "Del" key into the BIOS setup.
6. Press "F9" to load Optimal Defaults.
7. Press "F10" to save the changes and exit.
